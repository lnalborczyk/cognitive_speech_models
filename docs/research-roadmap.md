# Scientific development roadmap

The current public app retains only DIVA-inspired and Task Dynamics-inspired controllers. Priorities:

1. Establish calibration and repeatability of VTL canonical vowel acoustic targets, including uncertainty and missing resonances.
2. Benchmark zero-perturbation steady vowel stability across gains and delays, seeds and speaker models.
3. Study paired upward/downward auditory F1 shifts and quantify latency, direction, magnitude and recovery. Avoid interpreting these heuristics as human effect sizes.
4. Compare consonant target closures and release acoustics to independent VTL gestural-score trajectories, then EMA and acoustic recordings.
5. Replace the normalized articulatory actuator with richer biomechanical/task-space dynamics if quantitative predictions require it.
6. Train and validate model-specific forward and inverse sensory maps; separate feedback and feedforward contributions.

VTL acoustic synthesis is a research-based component, but full DIVA and Task Dynamics implementations and experimental fits have not been established here.
